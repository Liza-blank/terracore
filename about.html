About TerraCore

Builders in Northern Mindanao often send samples to labs far away, which means delays and extra cost. TerraCore brings materials testing closer to the job site.

Our mission

To give contractors, engineers, and government offices fast, accurate, standards-based test results that keep construction safe.

Our vision

To be the trusted local materials testing laboratory for infrastructure across Northern Mindanao.
